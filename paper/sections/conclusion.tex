\section{Conclusions and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a test bench with five interchangeable conflict resolution strategies and an oracle that counts lost
operations, and proposed a convergent type-aware hybrid strategy. The case study already separates the strategies: whole-record LWW loses edits even
without a real conflict and lets stale values win under clock skew, version vectors surface conflicts that a set or a
counter could merge, the CRDT libraries decide true conflicts silently, and the hybrid strategy reaches the ideal
outcome in all scenarios with less payload than LWW and Automerge, at the price of a larger state.

The next steps are the validation of the harness on public CRDT benchmark workloads, the full experiments E1--E6
with 30 seeds per configuration and the statistical tests of Section~\ref{sec:setup}, the reduction of the hybrid's
metadata, and the replay of a subset of the scenarios on emulators and a phone.
